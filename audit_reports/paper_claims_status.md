# Paper claims status (running file)

Paper: `../FACTS_MAS.pdf` (draft of 2026-09-26). Line numbers refer to its extracted text. Updated at the end of every phase. Status values:

- **holds**: the claim survives the audit so far.
- **weakened**: the claim is directionally supported but overstated, fragile, or based on contaminated evidence.
- **withdrawn**: the claim is false for the current system or rests on an invalid procedure; it must be removed or rewritten.
- **not yet checked**: scheduled for a later phase.

"R1" = current code with the seasonal window re-selected on validation (window 7). "R1c" = R1 + zero-support fallback + validation targets bounded to train_end (proposed baseline, Phase 2). CLEANBLEED = the 165 fold-6 cells with targets in Jan-Feb 2026 excluded. All are PROVISIONAL for T = 0.05, the Router design and the adaptive choice.

Last updated: Phase 6, 2026-10-16. FA-ETS-d = factor-adjusted damped ETS (window-7 factors), the best single model found in Phase 3. C2 = R1c with FA-ETS-d as the temporal channel.

## Proposed framing (Umang, 2026-10-16; pending co-author sign-off)

- **Production system** = the pre-registered Phase 3 rule outcome: R1c at 4 weeks, C2 at 8 and 13 weeks, with the event and intrinsic channels retired from the housing forecast path (kept in the tables as tested channels that calibrated to null).
- **FA-ETS-d** (factor-adjusted damped ETS) is reported as the strongest baseline. **No accuracy claim over FA-ETS-d unless Phase 4 shows statistical significance.**
- The method's contribution is the **protocol**: every channel tested against the strongest endogenous reference, calibration allowed to return a null, and recomposition of the forecast from the channels that survive.
- **Data cut:** Zillow's smoothed weekly series is primary; raw-series results go in the **main** results table, not an appendix. **The abstract must name the cut and give the gain over naive on both cuts** (Phase 3: 4-week reduction 49% smoothed vs 24% raw for R1c).
- **Attribution:** change-based attribution w_k (y_hat_k - y_t) is primary; level-based (eq. 11) moves to an appendix.
- **Phase 5 evidence for the protocol framing:** a new channel (Zillow listing flows: new listings, newly pending, price-cut share), tested against FA-ETS-d under pre-registered rules, returned a null (not promoted; worse than B5 in 3-4 of 6 folds). Delta fusion and temperature/shrinkage tuning also failed. Under the rules, production P stands at all horizons.
- **Known issue, not to be fixed:** the intrinsic channel's reversion schedule is horizon-inconsistent (F2.1). The channel is retired, so this is documented, not repaired.
