## Summary

1. **Fix 2** (fold weights for any regime with fewer than 13 distinct validation weeks) behaved as predicted. It changes exactly the 780 zero-support test cells: 8 weeks improves by 0.136, 13 weeks worsens by 0.204, 4 weeks is unchanged. The combined change (fix 2 + bounded validation targets, "R1c") gives **2.122 / 4.468 / 6.987** against R1's 2.089 / 4.952 / 7.058. That net gain rests mostly on one fold (fold 3 hiking), and 4 weeks gets slightly worse in 5 of 6 folds.
2. **The weight-ordering anomaly is explained.** Exogenous (macro) gets 0.169 mean weight at 13 weeks, and 0.137 of that comes from one regime cell: fold 3 hiking. There, 13 weeks of validation at the start of the only hiking episode gave AR a median skill of -0.60, so softmax handed macro 0.78. The remaining 0.032 is the equal-weight fallback. Under R1c macro's 13-week weight is 0.002.
3. **The regime story weakens further.** Under R1 the pooled fusion already beats naive in hiking and cutting at 13 weeks. The Router's cutting "correction" is entirely the fallback artifact, and in hiking the Router makes things worse. Its 13-week gain sits in stable-regime cells, and 60-75% of it comes from fold 4. That looks like horizon conditioning, not regime conditioning.
4. **The intrinsic channel is horizon-inconsistent** (strict-xfail test): the t+4 forecast pulls 0.30 of the gap if H = 4 and 0.028 if H = 13. The other four channels are consistent. The event channel equals naive in all 15,480 cells.
5. Under R1 a quarter of the 8- and 13-week weight (0.255 / 0.260) goes to naive or near-naive channels; under R1c, under 0.01. Level attribution (paper eq. 11) credits the event clone with 4.5-5.6% of the forecast even though it contributes nothing to the forecasted change.

## What surprised me and how I checked it

- **The combined fix improved 13 weeks**, against my prediction. I decomposed it by fold and regime before believing it. It is one fold gaining (fold 3 hiking, -0.273) and one fold losing (fold 5 cutting, +0.171). The fix is right on principle (do not trust 13 weeks of one episode, do not hand naive clones 60% of the weight), but I would not claim it improves accuracy in general: 1 of 6 folds improves at 13 weeks, 3 of 6 at 8 weeks, 1 of 6 at 4 weeks.
- **Under R1 the pooled fusion no longer fails in hiking or cutting.** The paper's regime narrative was built on the July system, where the unsmoothed seasonal channel was weak. Once the seasonal channel is decent, the failure the Router was built to fix is gone. I checked this with the Router turned off in both R1 and R1c.
- **The anomaly is fold 3 alone.** A full skill table per fold shows nothing unusual at fold level; only the regime-cell grid exposes it.

## Things I could not determine

- Whether the Router's 13-week gain is regime conditioning or horizon conditioning. The current ablation cannot separate them (F2.8). This needs a horizon-only, regime-blind weight set as the reference (proposed for Phase 4, not run).
- The source of the "7,830 origins, 17 of 18 cells" quadratic-schedule evidence (carried from Phase 1).
