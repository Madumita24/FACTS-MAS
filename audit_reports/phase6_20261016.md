## What surprised me and how I checked it

- **The 4-week gate gap.** I expected bit-identity and got 0.0007 at 4 weeks. I traced it to cross-environment optimizer differences in about 3% of Holt fits, and confirmed it by substitution: with cached Holt, every cell matches exactly.
- **In the UI domain the fused recipe beats FA-ETS-d at 8 and 13 weeks.** That is the opposite of housing and my prediction. Checks:
  - Per-state significance is thin (1-2 states after BH).
  - The seasonal channel alone nearly matches P-UI.
  - Validation picked undamped FA-ETS, so the composed temporal channel behaves differently in this domain.

  The cleanest reading is that strongly seasonal NSA claims favour the seasonal channel, and the fusion mostly rides it. A validation-selected single-model check for UI, the analogue of housing's SelBest, would settle whether fusion beats the best single model here. It was not pre-registered, so I did not run it (decision 3).
- **TimesFM's wall-clock time.** Explained by the battery sleep; checked against the event log and by parity of the output.
