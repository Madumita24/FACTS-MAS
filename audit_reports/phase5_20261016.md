## Summary

1. **Nothing beats FA-ETS-d, and no candidate is promoted.** The listing-flow channel (P5a), delta fusion (P5b) and temperature/shrinkage tuning (P5c) all fail the pre-registered rule. The best result, P5c at 4 weeks, is 0.002 behind FA-ETS-d (p = 0.42).
2. **The listing-flow channel is a clean null.** It used Zillow's smoothed new listings, newly pending and price-cut share, with a 2-week availability lag, and passed its leakage test. On its own it is about 1 MAPE point worse than FA-ETS-d. Inside the fusion it gets at most 4% weight and makes results slightly worse in 3-4 of 6 folds.
3. **Under the rules, the production system P stands at every horizon.** The Phase 5 base B5 (C2 with an FA-ETS-d skill reference) does not replace P. At 4 weeks B5 is better on test in 5 of 6 folds but loses on validation, the same pattern as Phase 3. At 8 and 13 weeks it is better in only 2 of 6 folds.
4. **Validation never chose shrinkage toward equal weights** (lambda = 0 in all 18 fold-horizon cells). The Phase 1 equal-weights observation does not generalise once FA-ETS-d is a channel.
5. **Raw-cut statistics (reporting only):**
   - P beats naive at every horizon (p <= 0.0001).
   - At 4 weeks, P is no better than the seasonal channel alone (p = 0.83) and is worse than FA-ETS-d (p = 0.003).
   - At 8 and 13 weeks, P and FA-ETS-d are not significantly different, but the intervals are too wide to show equivalence.

## What surprised me and how I checked it

- **Shrinkage was never chosen**, against my expectation and the Phase 1 equal-weights observation. That observation came from cells where the alternative was poorly estimated skill weights among weak channels. Once FA-ETS-d is a channel, equal weights over {FA-ETS-d, seasonal, macro} put a third of the weight on a near-naive channel, which validation rejects. Consistent across all 18 cells.
- **P5b overfits.** NNLS on validation gives the seasonal deviation real weight, and test punishes it. Validation itself also prefers B5 for P5b, so the rule rejects it on both counts.
- **P5a's validation wins are tiny** (0.004 and 0.016) and do not carry to test. This is the pattern the 5-of-6-folds rule exists to catch.
