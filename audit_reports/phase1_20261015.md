## Summary

1. **Confirmed: the seasonal smoothing window (5) was chosen using test data, including reserved-window data.** I rebuilt the lost sweep. It reproduces the original table to 3 decimals, but **only when the 165 Jan-Feb 2026 cells are included**.
2. **The whole move from the paper's 2.243 / 5.038 / 7.020 to 2.094 / 4.973 / 6.855 comes from that smoothing change.** July agents under today's harness reproduce the paper exactly. Removing the event magnitude changes nothing measurable (≤ 0.002).
3. **Re-selecting the window on validation only, nested by fold, picks 7 in every fold, not 5.** With window 7 (run "R1"), CLEANBLEED MAPE is 2.089 / 4.952 / 7.058, against 2.094 / 4.986 / 7.019 for PROVISIONAL. The 4- and 8-week gains survive clean selection; 13 weeks is 0.04 worse.
4. **New major finding:** when a regime has no validation data in a fold, the code gives every channel weight 0.2, including the two naive clones. This hits 390 test cells per horizon at 8 and 13 weeks (fold 2 hiking, fold 5 cutting). At 13 weeks these accidental equal weights beat skill weights, and they supply about 20% of the Router's 13-week advantage.
5. Bounding validation targets to `train_end` (F0.10) appears to improve 13-week MAPE by 0.39. **That gain is entirely the artifact in item 4**: one fold loses its last hiking validation points and falls back to equal weights. Excluding the 165 bleed cells raises pooled 13-week MAPE from 6.855 to 7.019 (naive: 11.168 to 10.814).

## What surprised me and how I checked it

- **F0.10 moved 13-week MAPE by 0.39**, four times my prediction. I did not believe it until I split it by fold and by validation support. One fold, one mechanism (the zero-support fallback) accounts for all of it. Cells with support in both versions moved +0.019. This is the clearest case so far of a "fix" that looks like a win for the wrong reason.
- **Validation chose window 7 everywhere.** I checked that the comparison is monotonic in every fold, not driven by one fold. Fold 1's early origins have under 53 weeks of history (flat factors, identical across windows), which dilutes but does not reverse the ordering. The chosen value sits on the grid edge (F1.13).
- **The bleed effect at 13 weeks (0.16) is larger than I expected.** Fold 6 has the lowest errors of all folds, so dropping its hardest-for-naive cells raises the pooled mean. Relative to naive, the reduction at 13 weeks falls from 38.6% to 35.1%.
- Process error, caught and corrected: PowerShell's `python` is a separate Python 3.13 install with pandas 2.3.3. A fixture run and a variant build were started on it. I stopped and reran both on the pinned interpreter; no number here comes from 3.13 (`phase1_log.md`).
