## Summary

1. **P beats naive decisively at every horizon.** Panel DM t = -6.9 / -6.3 / -5.7, p < 1e-4; significant in 15 of 15 MSAs after BH correction; the bootstrap CIs of the difference are far from 0.
2. **P is not better than FA-ETS-d at any horizon.** It is statistically equivalent at 8 weeks (within a 2% margin). At 13 weeks FA-ETS-d is marginally better: the bootstrap CI of the difference excludes 0, panel DM p = 0.09. At 4 weeks P is worse by 0.14 (DM p = 0.12; the 90% CI excludes 0). The decision "no accuracy claim over FA-ETS-d" stands.
3. **The Model Confidence Set separates little at 13 weeks.** At 90% it keeps every method except naive; only FA-ETS-d survives at 75%. At 8 weeks it keeps FA-ETS-d, P and C2 and drops R1c, EW-TS, horizon-only weights and Naive2.
4. **The macro "FFR p = 0.042" reproduces exactly but does not survive any re-check:** Holm 0.170, wild cluster bootstrap 0.073, Driscoll-Kraay 0.71. The distributed-lag pattern has the opposite signs to the sign-flip hypothesis and is not significant.
5. **The regime placebo finds no evidence that regime labels carry information.** 46% (8wk) and 27% (13wk) of 200 randomly block-permuted label sets do as well or better, and a regime-free recency-weighted skill beats the true regimes at 13 weeks (6.41 vs 6.99). Block-bootstrap CIs are 4-7x wider than iid ones, so any cell-level iid analysis would badly overstate precision.

## What surprised me and how I checked it

- **FA-ETS-d is marginally better than P at 13 weeks, not just tied.** I checked the direction in the DISCLOSURE run (same) and per MSA (no single MSA significant after BH; FA-ETS-d is lower in 14 of 15). The effect is small (0.07) and consistent, not driven by one place.
- **The MCS at 13 weeks keeps nearly everything.** 166 weeks with strongly shared shocks leave wide intervals. It is not a bug: the same data give clear separation at 8 weeks, and the paired DM tests at 13 weeks are significant for P vs R1c, HZ-weights and Naive2. The MCS's multiple-comparison control is simply more conservative.
- **The changes-based macro spec looked strongly significant** until the SEs allowed for cross-sectional dependence. This is exactly the failure mode the original analysis risked, so I report both and do not choose between specs after the fact.
- **Recency-weighted skill beat the true regimes by 0.58 at 13 weeks.** That is one pre-registered configuration, not a selection. I report it as evidence against the regime story, not as a candidate.
