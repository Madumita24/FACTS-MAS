# Phase 7 report: holdout test of the frozen system

Date: 2026-10-22. Branch `audit-oct2026`. Frozen configuration: `FINAL_CONFIG.md` (freeze commit `a27a23a`; runner sha256 `06dee8fd...`). Authorisation: FINAL_CONFIG 11.3. Step log: `phase7_log.md`. Tables: `results_tables/phase7_holdout_mape.csv`, `phase7_holdout_stats.csv`.

## Summary

1. **All four pre-registered hypotheses are supported** on the primary run (smoothed cut, lag 0):
   - **H7.1:** P beats naive at every horizon (4wk −1.88, 95% CI [−3.03, −0.90]; 8wk −3.52 [−5.68, −1.70]).
   - **H7.2:** FA-ETS-d beats naive.
   - **H7.3:** P is equivalent to FA-ETS-d at 8 weeks (90% CI [−0.029, +0.014], inside ±0.039).
   - **H7.4:** at 4 weeks FA-ETS-d is better than P (+0.105, 95% CI [−0.007, +0.182], DM p = 0.044).
   - 13-week results point the same way and are descriptive only.
2. **The backtest picture holds on new data.** The final system beats naive by about 60%. It does not beat the best single model, FA-ETS-d, at the confirmatory horizons.
3. **CoT (gpt-5.6-luna, out-of-sample by its documented cutoff) is competitive.**
   - It has higher MAPE than FA-ETS-d at every horizon, but the difference is not significant (p = 0.38 / 0.43 / 0.60).
   - TimesFM is significantly worse than FA-ETS-d at 4 and 8 weeks (p = 0.016, < 0.0001).
4. **Raw cut (secondary).**
   - P beats naive at every horizon and lag.
   - At 4 weeks P equals the seasonal channel (Naive2) to within 0.001.
   - At 8 weeks, lag 0, P is slightly better than FA-ETS-d (−0.038, 95% CI [−0.071, −0.003], p = 0.031). That is a secondary result, and Naive2 alone does as well, so it is not evidence for fusion.
5. **One runner bug.** The smoothed lag-2 statistics failed because 27 cells had no CoT forecast. Those statistics were computed post hoc under a rule recorded before viewing (FINAL_CONFIG 11.5). Two bugs in the CoT preparation code were found and fixed before any forecast call (11.4 items 5-6).

## What surprised me and how I checked it

- **CoT came close to the statistical models** (4wk 1.259 vs P 1.243). I checked three things:
  1. The model is out-of-sample by its documented cutoff. Its self-reported cutoff, "June 2024", is earlier still.
  2. Every response came from `gpt-5.6-luna`.
  3. The prompts contain only data dated on or before the information origin.

  The model may still have general knowledge of 2025-early 2026 housing trends from training. That is not leakage of holdout targets, but it should be disclosed.
- **On the raw cut P beat FA-ETS-d at 8 weeks.** P at 4 weeks equals Naive2, and at 8 weeks Naive2 alone (2.677) is as good as P (2.671). So this reflects which channel the fusion follows, not added value from fusing.
- **The smoothed lag-2 runner failure.** I traced it to the 15-MSA assertion with the exact missing cells (Miami, 10 origins).

## Things I could not determine

- Whether gpt-5.6-luna's training included any 2026 housing commentary beyond its documented cutoff (2026-02-16). Only the provider's statement is available.
- Why the CoT connection drop happened (local network or provider). The calls log shows 83 failed attempts within 11:28-11:30 and no HTTP status codes.

## Earlier conclusions I now revise

- The Phase 4 conclusion "P is not better than FA-ETS-d" holds out-of-sample. The 13-week descriptive point estimate flips sign (backtest FA-ETS-d better by 0.067; holdout P better by 0.035). Both differences are within the 2% margin, and neither is confirmatory.
- The Phase 6 conclusion that TimesFM loses to FA-ETS-d at every horizon holds on the holdout.
- The paper's CoT claim (fold 1, July system, gpt-4o-mini, inside the model's training window) is superseded by the holdout comparison: CoT is not better than FA-ETS-d.
